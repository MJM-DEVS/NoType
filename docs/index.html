How it works

Hold. Speak. Let go.

Works in email, chat, documents, your browser and your code editor. If you can place a cursor there, you can dictate into it.

1

Hold your shortcut

Ctrl + Shift + Space by default. A small overlay appears and reacts to your voice.

2

Speak naturally

Watch your words appear in the overlay while you talk. No need to say "comma" or "period".

3

Let go

Your text is cleaned up and typed where your cursor is. Prefer tapping? Press once to start, once to stop.

  • Reacts to your voice. Every style moves with the actual sound of what you say.
  • Seven accent colors, light and dark, with optional start and stop sounds.
  • Live preview in the settings. Place it at the bottom, top or center of your screen.
  • Fix it where you typed it. NoType notices the correction and asks once.
  • Catches variants: "Kuber Netis", "Kuber-Netis" and "Kubernetis" all become Kubernetes.
  • Your dictionary for names, brands and terms. Works with or without AI cleanup.
  • Rewrite anything: "make this more formal", "translate this to English", "as bullet points".
  • Or write something new. With nothing selected, it writes at your cursor.
  • Runs on your PC with a small local AI model. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Privacy

Your voice stays on your PC.

Dictation, AI cleanup and command mode work fully offline. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to trust us with.

No servers, no account

Speech recognition runs on your graphics card, the AI cleanup in a local engine. There is no NoType cloud.

Zero telemetry

No analytics, no usage tracking, no crash reports sent anywhere. The log file stays on your disk.

Online only when you ask

The speech model you pick, the optional AI engine and its updates. Everything else lives in %APPDATA%\NoType.

What you need

  • Windows 11, 64-bit
  • An NVIDIA graphics card (GTX 10 series or newer, ideally 6 GB video memory) for instant results. Without one, NoType runs on the processor with a smaller model.
  • About 2 GB of disk space, plus 0.1–3 GB for the speech model

"Windows protected your PC"?

NoType is free and not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen warns on first launch. Click More info → Run anyway. For the ZIP, right-click it first, open Properties, tick Unblock and then extract it. The download is large because it bundles NVIDIA's CUDA libraries, so you don't need to install anything else.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial, no subscription, no account. If NoType saves you time, a star on GitHub is the best way to say thanks.

Can I use it at work?

Yes. You can use NoType privately, at work, at school or anywhere else. The one thing the license doesn't allow is selling NoType, or a product or service built mainly on it.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only once, to download the speech model (and the AI engine, if you want it). After that NoType works fully offline.

I don't have an NVIDIA graphics card. Will it work?

Yes, NoType then runs on your processor. Choose the Small or Base model in the setup guide. Results take a little longer but are still very usable.

Which languages does it understand?

All 99 languages Whisper supports, with automatic detection if you like. German with English terms mixed in, or the other way around, works well with the large models. The app itself is available in English and German.

Does it work on macOS or Linux?

Not at the moment. NoType is built for Windows 11.

Where can I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Please open an issue on GitHub. If something breaks, the log file %APPDATA%\NoType\notype.log helps a lot.
